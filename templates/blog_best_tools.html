How to Choose the Right PDF Tool?

When choosing a PDF tool, consider these factors:

  • Privacy: Does the tool delete your files after processing?
  • Limits: Are there daily or size limits?
  • Signup: Do you need to create an account?
  • Watermarks: Does the tool add watermarks to your output?
  • Speed: How fast is the tool?
